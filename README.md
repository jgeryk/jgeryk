## AI Systems as a Lifelong Obsession

AI connects my interest in systems thinking with questions about intelligence and agency. **Agent orchestration** is one practical example: how shared context, tools, feedback, and human judgment shape the behavior of interacting agents. Understanding that behavior means moving between levels of abstraction, from individual model decisions to the organization of the larger workflow.

My interests in mathematics, history, language, and philosophy have developed together over many years, alongside my engineering work. Questions about formal models lead into questions about meaning; questions about intelligence lead into questions about agency and the social and historical contexts that shape those ideas.

I read **[Immanuel Kant](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/kant/)**, **[Georg Wilhelm Friedrich Hegel](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/hegel/)**, **[Alfred North Whitehead](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/whitehead/)**, their critics and successors, and other cross-disciplinary thinkers whose work serves as a lightning rod for fundamental questions about knowledge, nature, freedom, and experience. Those questions spill over into everything else, from how I understand a technical problem to how I make sense of ordinary life.

## Projects for fun

These are works in progress, mostly growing out of things I wanted to use myself.

**concept-relay** · *Learning from conversations*  
A local-first companion that turns concepts from AI conversations into editable flashcards, linked back to the evidence in the original conversation. It combines local model inference, a searchable concept library, and review before export.

**discogs-helper** · *Records and discovery*  
Imports collection data and gathers Discogs marketplace listings to help organize a record collection and find new music on physical formats. Vue/TypeScript, a Fastify API, and browser automation support filtering and ranking listings.

**homelab-hometown** · *A town for a homelab*  
An interface-builder concept for managing locally hosted software through a 2D, interactive, multiuser town. I’m exploring what that spatial metaphor could make easier to understand and use.

**vinyl-beatmatch** · *A closer look at DJ practice*  
An audio-analysis prototype that captures turntable or mixer inputs, displays waveforms and tempos, and assesses whether two streams are beatmatched. Session history and a local fingerprint catalog help recognize tracks and review practice sessions and sets.
